# PRODUCT.md

Subject: SignSense, a browser-based ASL fingerspelling practice coach that reads 21 hand landmarks and gives one actionable cue.
Audience: Hearing family members and new learners who want to learn between lessons, plus anyone curious about fingerspelling.
Primary job: Make it easy to begin a round, see one target shape, and know what to adjust next.
Voice: Direct, patient, and concrete; “Bring your thumb alongside your index finger.” The landing page begins with a family story, not a product feature list.
Not: A general chatbot, a dense analytics dashboard, or a substitute for Deaf teachers and ASL conversation.
Success: A visitor understands why a family would practice, then starts the camera or demo without hunting for it. A learner can tell when a handshape is ready and move to the next letter.
